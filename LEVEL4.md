## 📊 Features & Screens Overview

1. **Merchant POS Terminal**: Traditional instant register checkout.
2. **Yield Vault (PayVault)**: Allows locking cash balances into the yield contract, visualizing accrued yield, and harvesting yield.
3. **Anchor Settlement (SEP-24)**: Interactive simulated KYC and off-ramp flow to withdraw USD, EUR, and INR balances directly to local bank accounts.
4. **Diagnostics Telemetry**: Full system diagnostics logging, blocktime tracking, and transaction volume graphs.
5. **Feedback Feed**: Collects and displays onboarding user validation review cards.
